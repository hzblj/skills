Add the `unslop` skill (`skills/shared/unslop`): a writing pass that strips AI tells from prose and puts a human voice back in. Covers puffery, AI vocabulary, em dashes and connector colons, inline-header lists, chatbot artifacts, filler, abstract-metaphor jargon, and plain-speech rules (active voice, no adverb crutches, say what it does instead of how it feels), plus a common-mistakes table for over-correcting and a review checklist. Applies to READMEs, docs, commit messages, PR descriptions, and changelog entries.
